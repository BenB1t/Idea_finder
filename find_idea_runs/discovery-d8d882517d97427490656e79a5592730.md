## 1. Scorecard Sync: Structured Interview Tool for SMBs

**Potential user:** SMB hiring managers who conduct technical interviews without a standardized rubric.

**Problem:** Interviewers spend time manually reviewing resumes and taking notes, then struggle to compare candidates fairly, leading to extended hiring cycles and poor hires.

**Job to be done:** When I finish a technical interview, I want to quickly capture structured scores and see a side‑by‑side comparison, so I can decide faster and reduce bias.

**Potential buyer:** SMB HR leaders or founders who pay for hiring tools; they value reduced time‑to‑hire and lower cost‑per‑hire.

**Why software might help:** A lightweight web app can provide templates, real‑time score capture, and automatic aggregation, replacing spreadsheets and email threads.

**30-day success hypothesis:** Hypothesis: In a 30‑day pilot with 5 SMBs, the average time from interview to hiring decision decreases by at least 20%.

- The perceived effort to set up scorecard templates is low.

**Suggested first test:** Create a simple Google Form or Notion scorecard template, send it to 10 hiring managers, and ask them to use it for a week and report time spent.

## 2. On‑Demand Skills Matching for Contract Recruiters

**Potential user:** Contract recruiters or staffing agencies who need to quickly match freelancers or temporary workers to short‑term project slots.

**Problem:** Manual candidate sourcing and contract paperwork are time‑consuming, leading to delayed project starts and higher placement costs.

**Potential buyer:** Recruitment agencies that pay per placement; they value faster fills and lower administrative overhead.

**Why software might help:** A searchable talent pool with skill tags, availability calendars, and auto‑generated contracts can replace spreadsheets and email chains.

## 3. Personalized Job Application Automation for Engineers

**Potential user:** Software engineers actively applying to multiple positions and spending hours customizing resumes and cover letters.

**Problem:** Engineers repeat the same information across many applications, leading to fatigue, missed opportunities, and low response rates.

**Job to be done:** When I find a new job posting, I want to automatically generate a tailored resume and cover letter, so I can apply to more positions without burnout.

**Potential buyer:** Individual engineers willing to pay a monthly subscription for time savings, or career‑coaching platforms offering it as a value‑add.

**Why software might help:** AI‑driven template filling and document generation can drastically reduce manual editing while maintaining relevance.

**30-day success hypothesis:** Hypothesis: Users will increase their weekly application volume by at least 30% within 30 days, as measured by self‑reported counts.

**Evidence index:** 17.6/100 (signal strength only; not probability of success)
