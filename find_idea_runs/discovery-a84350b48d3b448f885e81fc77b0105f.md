**Potential buyer hypothesis:** Server owners and community managers who will pay a subscription for time savings and improved member retention

## 2. Limited Drop Authenticator

**Potential user hypothesis:** Independent artists selling limited‑edition anime prints, figures, and merchandise

**Problem hypothesis:** Verifying authenticity and preventing scalper fraud on resale markets is difficult, leading to lost revenue and brand trust

**Job to be done:** When I launch a limited drop, I want to issue tamper‑proof digital certificates and track resale transactions, so customers trust the authenticity and I can protect my brand value

**Potential buyer hypothesis:** Artists and small merch brands who will pay a per‑drop fee for authentication and analytics

**Why software might help:** Timestamped certificates provide immutable proof of authenticity, while resale tracking adds a data‑driven revenue stream for creators

**Analyst disposition:** inconclusive
**Research satisfied:** no after 3 round(s)
**Research assessment:** The supplied sources do not establish that scalper fraud or authenticity verification is a pain point for independent anime artists. The sources confirm artists exist, use commission platforms (VGen, Ko-Fi), and pay for business tools, but none mentions authentication challenges or resale fraud. Without evidence that the problem is real and felt by the target user, there is no foundation to assess willingness to pay for a solution. The opportunity hypothesis remains unvalidated.

**Current workarounds:**
- Artists use established platforms (VGen, Ko-Fi, Artistree, PayRequest) for commission management and payment processing; S1, S2, S3, S8 show these workarounds but none address authentication ([S1](https://www.instagram.com/p/DWq732LjDF6/), [S2](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdwN68_sNdz/), [S3](https://panpanartcomms.carrd.co/), [S8](https://payrequest.io/for/commissions))

**Alternatives and pricing:**
- Multiple commission platforms exist (VGen, Ko-Fi, Artistree, Foundmyself) competing for artist business; S4 compares fees and traffic quality across platforms ([S4](https://www.foundmyself.com/review/?srsltid=AU7gw4WY1pX_6oIkxlegMy0gtKzFEpRA0vclxrE4urfsAg-Jb0RTpGH7), [S7](https://www.facebook.com/groups/1106838863654712/posts/1794750551530203/))
- Artist business tools (client management, invoicing) range $0-$49/month; S10 shows artists pay for workflow software but no authentication-specific pricing ([S10](https://agiled.app/blog/best-client-management-software-for-artists))

**Willingness-to-pay evidence (not_found):** No direct evidence that a buyer pays for this specific workaround was found.

**Still unknown:**
- Do independent anime artists actually experience scalper fraud or authenticity disputes on resale markets?
- What is the current behavior when authenticity is questioned—do artists or buyers absorb the cost?
- Would artists pay a per-drop fee for authentication, or would free alternatives (handwritten signatures, photo proofs) suffice?
- What platforms or methods do artists currently use for limited drops?
- Do any existing competitors offer authentication specifically for independent artists?
- Reached the research-round limit before the source-backed satisfaction conditions were met.

**Assumptions to check:**
- Artists have sufficient margin to afford a per‑drop fee
- Buyers care enough about authenticity to seek verification
- Integration with existing e‑commerce platforms can be implemented without heavy development

**Suggested first test:** Interview artists about experiences with fraud and interest in a tool that issues digital authenticity certificates for limited releases
